Baysor cell segmentation statistics (`segmentation_cell_stats.csv`, gzipped) for two Cartana in situ sequencing sections of sham-operated mouse kidney from GEO:

- `GSM7090410_cartana_sham_male_segmentation_cell_stats.csv.gz`: male, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM7090410
- `GSM7090415_cartana_sham_female_segmentation_cell_stats.csv.gz`: female, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM7090415

Cell centroids (`x`, `y`) are in image pixels.
